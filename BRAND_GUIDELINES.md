# Talery Brand Guidelines

## Brand Overview

Talery is an AI-powered social travel platform where travel creators, influencers, local experts, and travelers discover, create, share, and monetize travel itineraries.

**Vision:** Become the "YouTube + Airbnb + Canva + Spotify for Travel."

## Brand Personality

- Premium
- Modern
- Minimal
- Luxury
- Friendly
- Innovative
- Adventure
- Community-driven
- AI-first

## Color Palette

### Primary
- **Sky Blue:** `#0EA5E9`
- Usage: Primary buttons, links, accents, hero elements

### Secondary
- **Teal:** `#14B8A6`
- Usage: Secondary buttons, success states, badges

### Accent
- **Orange:** `#F97316`
- Usage: CTAs, highlights, premium badges, warm moments

### Neutrals
- **Background:** White (`#FFFFFF`) / Dark `#020617`
- **Foreground:** Slate 900 `#0F172A` / Dark `#F8FAFC`
- **Muted:** Slate 100 `#F1F5F9` / Dark `#1E293B`
- **Border:** Slate 200 `#E2E8F0` / Dark `#1E293B`

## Typography

- **Primary Font:** Geist Sans (via Next.js Google Font)
- **Monospace:** Geist Mono
- **Headlines:** Bold, tight tracking (`tracking-tight`)
- **Body:** Relaxed line-height, readable contrast

## Design Principles

1. **Glassmorphism** — Use `backdrop-blur` with translucent backgrounds
2. **Soft Shadows** — Elevated cards with subtle shadows
3. **Modern Gradients** — Sky-to-teal gradients for brand moments
4. **Rounded Corners** — `rounded-2xl` and `rounded-3xl` for cards and buttons
5. **Large Spacing** — Generous whitespace for premium feel
6. **Motion** — Subtle Framer Motion animations on scroll and hover

## Voice & Tone

- Warm and inviting like Airbnb
- Confident and polished like Apple
- Clear and simple like Notion
- Energetic and innovative like Linear

## Logo Usage

- Logo combines a plane icon inside a rounded gradient square (sky-to-teal)
- Wordmark: "Talery" in bold, tight tracking
- Always maintain clear space around the logo

## Imagery Style

- Cinematic travel photography
- Vibrant destinations
- Authentic moments over staged stock
- Diverse travelers and creators
- Aerial landscapes and cityscapes

## Do's and Don'ts

**Do:**
- Use ample whitespace
- Keep layouts clean and minimal
- Use gradients sparingly for emphasis
- Ensure strong contrast for accessibility

**Don't:**
- Clutter layouts
- Use too many colors
- Use low-quality images
- Compromise readability for decoration

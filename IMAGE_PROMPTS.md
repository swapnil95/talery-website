## Illustration Style Guide

- Prefer 3D renders with soft shadows over flat illustrations
- Use glassmorphism and translucent layers
- Maintain the brand color palette
- Keep compositions clean and uncluttered
- Use cinematic lighting for photography
- Show diversity in people and destinations
